CV & Cover Letter Design

Help job seekers stand out while building a profitable service business

Overview

CV and cover letter design is a service that helps job seekers present themselves professionally. Many people struggle to create attractive, ATS-friendly CVs and are willing to pay for expert assistance.

Why This Business Works

  • Very low startup costs
  • Can be done entirely online
  • High demand among job seekers and graduates
  • Can expand into LinkedIn profile optimization
  • No physical inventory required

Startup Cost

  • Internet connection: Existing or ± R200/month
  • Laptop or computer: Existing or second-hand
  • Canva Pro (optional): ± R150/month
  • Microsoft Office or Google Docs

Estimated startup cost: R0 – R500

Tools You Need

  • Canva
  • Microsoft Word
  • Google Docs
  • Email account
  • WhatsApp Business
  • Cloud storage (Google Drive)

Services You Can Offer

  • Professional CV creation
  • CV redesign and upgrades
  • Cover letter writing
  • LinkedIn profile optimization
  • Job application assistance
  • Graduate starter packages

Pricing Guide

  • Basic CV: R50 – R150
  • Professional CV: R150 – R350
  • CV + Cover Letter: R250 – R500
  • LinkedIn Optimization: R200 – R400
  • Premium Package: R500 – R1000+

Income Potential

Example:

  • 3 CVs per day × R200 = R600/day
  • 5 working days per week = R3 000/week
  • Monthly potential = ± R12 000/month

Experienced designers with strong reputations can earn significantly more.

How To Get Clients

  • Advertise on WhatsApp Status
  • Join local job-seeker Facebook groups
  • Offer free CV reviews
  • Create TikTok career tips content
  • Partner with colleges and training centers
  • Ask happy clients for referrals

Growth Opportunities

  • Career coaching
  • Interview preparation
  • Business proposal writing
  • Company profiles
  • Professional document formatting
  • Freelance writing services
