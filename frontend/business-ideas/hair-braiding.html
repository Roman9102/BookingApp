Hair Braiding / Weaves Business

Start, grow, and scale a profitable beauty service business

Overview

Hair braiding is a high-demand beauty service focused on protective hairstyles such as braids, twists, cornrows, and weaves. It is popular because it is affordable, long-lasting, and culturally significant in many communities.

Startup Requirements

  • Basic braiding skills (practice on mannequins or friends)
  • Hair extensions (kanekalon, synthetic hair)
  • Combs, clips, edge control, and gel
  • Mirror + clean workspace
  • Mobile phone for bookings and marketing

Startup Cost Estimate

Low budget setup: R300 – R1000
Medium setup (with stock + branding): R1000 – R3000

Pricing Strategy

  • Simple braids: R80 – R150
  • Box braids: R200 – R600
  • Ghana braids: R150 – R400
  • Custom styles: R300+

How to Get Clients

  • Post before/after pictures on WhatsApp & Instagram
  • Offer first-time discounts
  • Partner with local salons or friends
  • Use Quick Connect to list your services

Growth Path

Once stable, you can expand into wig installation, hair treatment, and mobile salon services. Eventually, you can register as a full service provider on the platform.
