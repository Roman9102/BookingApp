Delivery Services Business

Build a reliable delivery business serving individuals and local businesses

Overview

Delivery services help customers transport food, parcels, groceries, documents, and other items quickly and safely. With online shopping and convenience becoming increasingly important, delivery businesses continue to grow across cities and communities.

Why This Business Works

  • Growing demand for fast deliveries
  • Can start small and scale gradually
  • Flexible working hours
  • Potential partnerships with local businesses
  • Recurring customers and contracts

Startup Cost

  • Bicycle (optional): R1 000 – R5 000
  • Motorbike: R10 000 – R40 000+
  • Fuel budget
  • Delivery backpack or storage box
  • Smartphone with internet access

Estimated startup cost: R2 000 – R50 000+

Equipment Needed

  • Smartphone
  • Reliable transportation
  • Power bank
  • Delivery bag or container
  • Reflective safety gear
  • GPS navigation app

Services You Can Offer

  • Food delivery
  • Parcel delivery
  • Document delivery
  • Grocery delivery
  • Pharmacy pickups
  • Same-day local deliveries

Pricing Guide

  • Short distance: R30 – R60
  • Medium distance: R60 – R120
  • Long distance: R120 – R300+
  • Business contracts: Negotiated rates

Income Potential

Example:

  • 10 deliveries per day × R50 = R500/day
  • 6 days per week = R3 000/week
  • Monthly potential = ± R12 000/month

Business contracts and multiple drivers can significantly increase revenue.

How To Get Customers

  • Partner with local restaurants
  • Approach grocery stores
  • Advertise on WhatsApp groups
  • Create Facebook business pages
  • Offer first-delivery discounts
  • Provide excellent customer service

Growth Opportunities

  • Hire additional drivers
  • Create a local delivery fleet
  • Offer business delivery contracts
  • Expand to neighboring areas
  • Develop a booking app or website
  • Partner with e-commerce sellers
