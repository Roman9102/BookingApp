Event Decor & Backdrop Business

Create memorable celebrations and turn creativity into a profitable business

Overview

Event decor businesses provide decorations, backdrops, balloon setups, table styling, and themed event designs for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, graduations, corporate events, and more.

Why This Business Works

  • High demand throughout the year
  • Customers pay premium prices for quality setups
  • Many items can be reused for multiple events
  • Easy to grow through referrals and social media
  • Can start from home with a small inventory

Startup Cost

  • Backdrop stand: R800 – R2 500
  • Balloons & accessories: R500 – R2 000
  • Table cloths & decor items: R1 000 – R5 000
  • Lighting (optional): R500 – R3 000
  • Transport costs

Estimated startup cost: R2 000 – R15 000+

Materials & Equipment

  • Backdrop stands
  • Balloon pump
  • Balloon garland kits
  • Artificial flowers
  • Table runners & covers
  • Decorative props
  • Lighting equipment

Services You Can Offer

  • Birthday party decor
  • Wedding decor
  • Baby shower setups
  • Graduation celebrations
  • Corporate event styling
  • Balloon arches & garlands
  • Custom backdrops

Pricing Guide

  • Basic balloon setup: R500 – R1 500
  • Backdrop package: R1 500 – R4 000
  • Baby shower decor: R2 000 – R6 000
  • Wedding decor: R5 000 – R30 000+
  • Corporate events: Custom pricing

Income Potential

Example:

  • 2 events per weekend × R3 000 = R6 000
  • 4 weekends per month = R24 000/month

Larger weddings and corporate events can generate significantly more income.

How To Get Clients

  • Post setup photos on Instagram and Facebook
  • Create TikTok transformation videos
  • Partner with photographers
  • Work with event planners
  • Offer discounts for referrals
  • Build a professional portfolio

Growth Opportunities

  • Event planning services
  • Equipment rentals
  • Wedding coordination
  • Corporate event management
  • Luxury decor packages
  • Full event production services
