Beard Grooming Business

Modern grooming skills turned into a profitable service business

Overview

Beard grooming is a personal care service focused on shaping, trimming, and maintaining beards. It can be offered as a mobile service, at home setups, or small barbershop-style businesses.

Why This Business Works

  • High demand for male grooming services
  • Low startup cost compared to barbershops
  • Repeat customers every 1–2 weeks
  • Can be done mobile (house calls)

Startup Cost

Basic tools setup: ± R300 – R1200

  • Clipper set
  • Trimmer
  • Beard scissors
  • Comb & brush
  • Aftershave / beard oil

Services You Can Offer

  • Beard trimming & shaping
  • Line-ups / edge clean-ups
  • Beard wash & conditioning
  • Hot towel grooming
  • Full grooming packages

Pricing Guide

  • Basic beard trim: R50 – R100
  • Full grooming: R100 – R200
  • Premium package: R200 – R350

Income Potential

5 clients per day × R100 average = R500/day
6 days a week = ± R12 000/month

How To Start

  • Practice on friends and family
  • Take before & after photos
  • Post on WhatsApp & TikTok
  • Offer discounted first cuts
  • Build a small client base first
