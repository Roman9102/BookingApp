Mobile Nail Technician Business

Academic & Practical Guide to Starting a Nail Tech Business

Overview

A mobile nail technician business provides professional nail care services at clients’ homes, workplaces, or events. It is a flexible, low-entry-cost business ideal for entrepreneurs who want to start in the beauty industry without needing a physical salon.

Core Services

  • Manicures (basic, gel, acrylic)
  • Pedicures and foot care
  • Nail art and custom designs
  • Press-on nail installations
  • Wedding / event nail styling

Startup Requirements

Estimated startup cost: Low to Medium

  • Nail kit (UV lamp, drill, files, brushes)
  • Gel & acrylic products
  • Sanitizers & hygiene equipment
  • Portable table & chair setup
  • Transport (for mobile services)

Suggested Pricing

  • Basic manicure: R80 – R150
  • Gel nails: R150 – R300
  • Acrylic sets: R250 – R500
  • Nail art (add-on): R30 – R120
  • Mobile service fee: R50 – R150

Skills Needed

  • Attention to detail
  • Creativity and design sense
  • Customer service skills
  • Basic hygiene and sanitation knowledge

Business Growth Ideas

  • Offer bridal packages
  • Partner with hair stylists & makeup artists
  • Sell nail products (press-ons, oils)
  • Create social media portfolio
