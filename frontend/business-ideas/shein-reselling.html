Shein Reselling Business

Modern guide to building a fashion reselling brand

Overview

Shein reselling involves purchasing trendy, affordable clothing from Shein and reselling it locally at a higher price. It is popular because fast fashion is always in demand, especially among young customers.

What You Can Sell

  • Dresses & outfits
  • Two-piece sets
  • Jeans & tops
  • Jackets & hoodies
  • Accessories (bags, jewelry, belts)

Startup Requirements

Estimated startup cost: R300 – R5000

  • Initial Shein order stock
  • Measuring tape & sizing guide
  • Packaging materials
  • Social media pages (TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp)

Pricing Strategy

  • Markup: 50% – 200% depending on item demand
  • High-demand outfits = higher profit margins
  • Bundle deals (outfit + accessories)

Marketing Strategy

  • Try-on haul videos on TikTok
  • Instagram fashion reels
  • WhatsApp catalog daily updates
  • Influencer collaborations

Growth Opportunities

  • Create your own clothing brand
  • Start a mini boutique store
  • Become a fashion influencer
  • Expand into dropshipping systems
