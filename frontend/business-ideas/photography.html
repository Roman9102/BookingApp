Photography Business

Academic & Practical Guide to Starting a Photography Business

Overview

A photography business involves capturing professional images for events, individuals, brands, and products. It is a creative and high-income skill that can start small and grow into a full studio or agency.

Core Services

  • Event photography (weddings, parties, funerals)
  • Portrait photography
  • Product photography for businesses
  • Social media content shoots
  • Graduation & lifestyle shoots

Startup Requirements

Estimated startup cost: Medium to High

  • Camera (DSLR or mirrorless)
  • Lenses (portrait & zoom)
  • Lighting equipment
  • Editing software (Lightroom / Photoshop)
  • Tripod & memory cards

Suggested Pricing

  • Mini shoot: R300 – R800
  • Event coverage: R800 – R3000+
  • Wedding packages: R3000 – R15000+
  • Product shoots: R50 – R200 per item

Skills Needed

  • Composition & lighting knowledge
  • Editing and retouching
  • Creativity and storytelling
  • Client communication

Business Growth Ideas

  • Create a photography brand portfolio
  • Offer video content services
  • Sell presets and editing packs
  • Partner with event planners
